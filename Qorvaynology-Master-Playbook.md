Most new agencies sell "we do everything with AI, cheap and fast". We sell the opposite:

**A small product studio that builds and ships its own products, and builds yours with the same care.** AI is a tool we use, never the pitch.

  2. One team for the whole job: brand, design, build, marketing.

## PART C: THE $10,000/MONTH PLAN

### C1. The goal

**$10,000 in revenue in the first month (16 Nov – 15 Dec 2026), and every month after.** Ryan is full-time on this, the team builds real websites fast with AI as a tool, and we go after **a few big clients instead of many small ones**.

Why big clients: 4–5 clients at $2,000–$3,000 each are easier to handle, deliver and keep happy than 20 clients at $500, and they turn into bigger retainers.

**Cash note:** with 50% upfront, $10,000 of signed work brings in about $5,000–7,000 cash inside the month, depending on delivery. To *collect* $10,000 this month, sign about **$14,000** of work, deliver fast, or ask for 60–70% upfront on short projects.

### C3. The funnel for big clients

To win 4–5 big clients in 30 days:

- **~250 qualified outreach messages and high-budget proposals** (businesses and startups that can pay $2,000+), about 3 people × 4 messages a day, every day
- → **~25 replies** (1 in 10)
- → **~12 discovery calls**
- → **~8 proposals**
- → **4–5 clients** (1 in 2 proposals)

**Qualified** means they can pay: funded startups, established companies, businesses running ads, agencies abroad, brands with real sales. Skip anyone asking for a $50 website.

Track every Friday: messages sent, replies, calls, proposals, clients, revenue.

### C4. How we win big clients fast

1. **Speed as a selling point.** "Your new website live in 7 days, not 2 months." We can do it because we build fast; price on value, not on hours.
2. **Free first impression.** Before the call, build a quick homepage mockup or a 1-page audit for the prospect. Nothing sells like seeing their own business looking better.
3. **Show the build.** Reels and LinkedIn posts that show real builds attract inbound leads (see Part D4).
4. **Upwork filters:** only bid on jobs with $1,000+ budgets or $30+/hour, verified payment, and good client history.
5. **Retainer on every project.** After launch, offer growth or care so revenue repeats next month.
6. **Referrals.** Every happy client gets asked for 1–2 introductions.

**Local business (Bangla or English):**
> Assalamu alaikum! I'm [name] from Qorvaynology, a design and tech studio from Kushtia. We're Qorvaynology, a small studio that builds its own products (like Orbyn, a live web app). I sketched a quick idea for how [page/product] could work better. Worth sending over?

**Follow-up (day 3, then day 7):**
> Hi [Name], just checking if you saw my note about [Company]'s [website/app]. Happy to share the free idea, no obligation.

**Rules:** personal, short, about their problem; give something first; 2 follow-ups max; expect about 1 reply in 10.

### E4. Sales call and proposal

1. **Call (20–30 min):** goals, problems, budget, deadline, who decides. Listen more than talk. Repeat their problem back.
2. **Proposal within 24 hours:** their problem → our solution → timeline → 3 price tiers → next step.
3. **Close:** contract + 50% deposit to start.

## PART F: HOW WE RUN PROJECTS

1. Discovery call (free)
2. Proposal with 3 tiers (within 24 hours)
3. Contract + 50% deposit
4. Kickoff: shared folder, chat group, timeline
5. Weekly update every Friday
6. Delivery, 2 revision rounds, final payment
7. Handover, 30 days support, review request, retainer or care-plan offer, referral ask

**Contract covers:** scope, price, payment schedule, timeline, revisions, ownership after payment, permission to show the work.

**Getting paid:** Payoneer for international platforms (PayPal doesn't work for receiving in Bangladesh); bKash or bank for local clients. Check the government's freelancer remittance incentive with the bank.

**Team rules (draft):** one lead per project who owns the client and deadline · 20% of each project to the agency (tools, ads, savings), 80% to the people who did the work · everything tracked in one shared sheet · Slack for team talk, Google Drive for files, Figma for design, GitHub for code.

**Never lose work:** keep everything in the cloud (GitHub, Figma, Google Drive, Behance), not on laptops. Save screenshots and a short write-up before deleting any client files.
