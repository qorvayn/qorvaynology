## 1. What Qorvaynology is

Qorvaynology is a design and technology agency (a "studio") run by members of the **QORVAYN** team. It designs, builds and grows digital products for other businesses: brands, websites, web apps, e-commerce stores, AI automation and social media growth.

- **Parent company:** QORVAYN, a technology company founded on 10 March 2026 in Bangladesh by Ryan Zannah (Founder & CEO), with co-founders Adelia Maharani and Siyami Muntaha Saba.
- **Status:** brand new, being set up in November 2026. No clients yet.
- **Location:** Bangladesh (Dhaka). Remote-first, no physical office.
- **Markets:** local Bangladeshi businesses and international clients (via Upwork, Contra, Fiverr, LinkedIn and direct outreach).

## 2. The name and what it means

**Qorvaynology = QORVAYN + -ology** ("-ology" is Greek for "the study or science of", as in technology and biology).

Meaning: **"the QORVAYN way of building technology"**: the method and craft QORVAYN uses to build its own products, now offered to other businesses.

Ready-made answers:
- **One line:** "Qorvaynology means *the science of how QORVAYN builds*: our method, now available for your business."

Qorvaynology is that knowledge turned into a service. When you work with us, you're not hiring freelancers. You're getting the method behind our own products."

## 3. Positioning: how we are different

Most new agencies sell "we do everything with AI, cheap and fast". Qorvaynology sells the opposite:

**A small product studio that builds and ships its own products, and builds yours with the same care.** AI is a tool we use, never the pitch.

**One-line promise (draft):** "We design, build and grow digital products for businesses that want to look world-class, made by a team that ships its own."

What makes it believable:
- **We run real products:** Orbyn (live web app), SnowVayn (clothing brand), the QORVAYN ecosystem.
- **One team for the whole job:** brand, design, website or app, and marketing setup.
- **Strategy first, then build:** every project starts with discovery (goals, users, competitors).
- **Craft over volume:** fewer clients, deeper work, clear process, on-time delivery. No generic templates.
- **Measurable results:** launch checklist and 30 days of support on every project.

**Never say:** "cheap", "fast AI", "we do everything", "100% satisfaction", long tool lists, invented statistics, fake reviews or fake client names.

## 9. Where we find clients

**Platforms:** Upwork (agency account, high priority), Contra (0% fee, high), LinkedIn (company page + personal profiles, high), Fiverr (medium), Behance + Dribbble (medium), later Toptal/Arc.dev, Clutch/GoodFirms.

**Direct outreach (best for big clients):**
- LinkedIn: founders, CEOs, marketing managers
- Cold email: businesses abroad with weak websites (personal messages only, never mass email)
- Google Maps: local Dhaka businesses with no website or a weak one
- Facebook/Instagram brands, Facebook business groups, X/Twitter founders, Reddit/Indie Hackers
- White-label partnerships with other agencies
- Personal network and referrals

**Target "big fish":** funded startups, established local businesses with bad websites (clinics, real estate, restaurants, schools, interior design, travel), small agencies abroad that outsource, growing Bangladeshi brands.

**Outreach rules:** short, personal, about their problem; give something first (mini audit or redesign idea); follow up twice (day 3 and day 7); expect about 1 reply in 10.

**Message template:**
> Hi [Name], I came across [Business] and liked [something specific]. I noticed [specific problem], which may be losing you customers. We're Qorvaynology, a small studio in Dhaka. We built [Orbyn / link]. I made a quick idea for how it could look. Can I send it over? — [Name]

## 10. How we work with clients

1. Discovery call (free, 15–30 min)
2. Proposal with 3 tiers within 24 hours
3. Contract + 50% deposit
4. Kickoff: shared folder, chat group, timeline
5. Weekly update every Friday
6. Delivery, 2 revision rounds, final payment
7. Handover, 30 days support, review request, retainer offer

Contracts cover scope, price, payment schedule, timeline, revisions, ownership after payment, and permission to show the work in our portfolio.

**Payments:** Payoneer for international platforms (PayPal does not work for receiving in Bangladesh). bKash/bank for local clients.

**Team split (draft):** each project has one lead; 20% to the agency (tools, ads, savings), 80% to the people who did the work.
